Q.2: Measurement Scales in Education (Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, Ratio)

Key Definitions & Practical School Examples:

  • Nominal: Categorical identification (e.g. Student Gender, Board Name).
  • Ordinal: Ranked orders without equal intervals (e.g. 1st, 2nd, 3rd position in class).
  • Interval: Equal distance between score points without an absolute zero (e.g. Test scores in Percentage, IQ scales).
  • Ratio: True zero point with proportional relations (e.g. Study hours per week, attendance count).
